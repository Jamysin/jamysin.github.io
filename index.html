👋 What Is agents-dont-sleep?

Ever worked with AI coding agents like Claude Code, Codex, or Gemini CLI and wished they could keep going while you walk away? These tools need your computer to stay awake—but you don't want to keep the screen on or risk draining your battery.

agents-dont-sleep is a tiny, smart tray app that sits quietly in your system tray (near the clock). It does one simple, genius job: It keeps your laptop awake only when your AI agents are working. When they finish, it lets your laptop sleep naturally. You close the lid, put it in your bag, and come back to finished tasks.

🔄 How Does It Work? (Simple Version)

Imagine your laptop as a factory. Normally, closing the lid = factory closed. agents-dont-sleep creates a smart power cord for your AI agents:

  • 🔍 It watches for signs that Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Gemini CLI, or similar tools are running.
  • ⚡ When they are active, it sends a harmless "stay awake" signal to your computer.
  • 😴 Once they're done and idle, it releases the signal and lets your laptop sleep normally.
  • 🪟 It works alongside your normal sleep settings—only overrides them when agents need it.

✨ Why You'll Love It

This little tool is a game-changer for AI users:

  • 🚀 Never Lose Progress: AI coding sessions that run for minutes or hours won't crash because your laptop fell asleep.
  • 🔋 Save Battery: It doesn't keep your laptop awake forever—just when agents work. Your screen stays off, so you use less power than normal.
  • 🎯 Perfect for Commutes: Close the lid, put your laptop in a bag, walk to work. Agents keep processing. No more waiting at your desk.
  • 🛠️ Works with All Major Agents: Built for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Gemini CLI, Cursor, and many others.
  • 🪶 Super Light: Uses next-to-no memory (less than 50 MB RAM) and 0% CPU when idle.
  • 🌍 Cross-Platform: Available for macOS (menu bar), Windows (system tray), and Linux (tray icons).
  • 🔥 Built with Modern Tech: Crafted in Rust and React using Tauri—so it's blazing fast and secure.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❔ Is it safe for my laptop?

Yes! It only prevents sleep—no changes to your files, settings, or hardware. When agents are idle, your laptop sleeps exactly as before.

❔ Will it drain my battery?

Not much more than running the AI agent itself. The screen stays off, so your battery lasts longer than if you kept the screen on. The app itself uses almost no power.

❔ How do I know it's working?

Open the tray menu—you'll see a green dot with "Monitoring" and a list of active agents. If you want a quick test, start an AI agent and watch the app switch to "Keeping awake" (yellow dot).

❔ Can I use it with tools other than coding agents?

Currently it's optimized for coding agents. The list includes Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Cursor, Octagon, Aider, Fabric, and many more. New tools get added regularly.
